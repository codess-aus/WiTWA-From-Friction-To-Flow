# From Friction to Flow

**The confidence to build what's next**

*Women in Tech WA 2026 Keynote by Michelle Sandford, Microsoft*

Look at the little diver in the picture. Helmet sealed. Heavy suit. Every breath coming through a tube.

For Michelle, that picture is a worst nightmare. Not the water, but the mask: the enclosed space, the feeling of losing control. And that is how a lot of people feel about AI right now. **It is not the technology that scares us. It is the feeling of losing control.**

Most ideas do not die from a lack of talent. They drown in friction. But look again. This diver is not sinking. She is swimming up, toward the light.

Brave is not the absence of fear. Brave is going in anyway.

This is not a story about AI. **AI is not the hero of this story. You are.** Because the future is not built by the people who felt ready. It is built by the people who started anyway.

This is the companion site to the keynote. It is a story told in twelve short chapters, each one matching a slide from the talk. Read them in order, or jump straight to the one that speaks to you.

You do not need to be a programmer to be here. You only need an idea that is still waiting for its first version.
